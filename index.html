How do cats know if they're going to fit into spaces?

Cats wiskers are about the same width as their body! Cats use their whiskers to see if they can fit through tight spaces before going in.

Can cats taste sugar?

No cats cannot taste sugar. Though many cats may enjoy sugary treats they do not have the taste buds to know that it is sweet.
